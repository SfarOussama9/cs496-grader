# Corvina Airways - Customer Policy Handbook (v1.0)

> FICTIONAL DOCUMENT created for CS496 Project 1. Corvina Airways is not a real airline.
> Rules and amounts deliberately differ from real-world airline norms, so a model that
> answers from memory instead of from this text can be detected.

## S01 Scope and definitions

1. This handbook applies to all flights marketed and operated by Corvina Airways (flight numbers beginning with CV). Corvina Airways is based at Tunis-Carthage airport (TUN).
2. On flights marketed by Corvina but operated by a partner airline (codeshare flights), the operating airline's rules apply to baggage, delays, cancellations and denied boarding. Corvina's rules on changes and refunds (Section S05) still apply to tickets purchased from Corvina.
3. Flight distance bands:
   - Short-haul: under 1,500 km.
   - Medium-haul: from 1,500 km to 3,500 km.
   - Long-haul: more than 3,500 km.
4. Arrival delay is measured as the difference between the scheduled arrival time and the time the aircraft door is opened at the destination gate.
5. "Departure" means the scheduled departure time printed on the booking confirmation.
6. Fare families: Light, Classic and Flex (economy cabin), and Business.
7. Age categories, on the date of travel: infant (under 2 years), child (2 to 11 years), adult (12 years and over).

## S02 Fare families and booking

1. Light: one personal item only. No cabin bag and no checked bag are included. No changes are allowed and the fare is not refundable (see S05 for taxes). A seat is assigned free of charge at check-in; seat selection can be purchased (S15).
2. Classic: one personal item, one cabin bag and one checked bag of up to 27 kg. Changes and refunds are allowed for a fee (S05).
3. Flex: one personal item, one cabin bag and two checked bags of up to 27 kg each. Free standard seat selection. Free changes and full refunds (S05).
4. Business: one personal item, one cabin bag of up to 12 kg and two checked bags of up to 32 kg each. All seats free. Lounge access. Free changes and full refunds (S05).
5. 24-hour free cancellation: a booking made at least 7 days before departure may be cancelled free of charge for a full refund within 24 hours of purchase, on all fare families. This does not apply to bookings made less than 7 days before departure.
6. Name corrections: a correction of up to 3 characters in a passenger's name is free if requested at least 24 hours before departure. Tickets cannot be transferred to another person.
7. Upgrading: a passenger may move to a higher fare family by paying the fare difference at the time of the upgrade, up to 3 hours before departure.
8. Downgrading: moving to a lower fare family is treated as a change under S05. Any fare difference is not refunded.

## S03 Cabin baggage

1. Personal item: maximum 40 x 30 x 15 cm and must fit under the seat in front. All passengers, including Light, may bring one personal item.
2. Cabin bag: maximum 55 x 35 x 20 cm and 9 kg (Business: 12 kg). Included in Classic, Flex and Business.
3. Light passengers may add a cabin bag online for EUR 20 per flight, up to 6 hours before departure.
4. A Light passenger who presents a cabin bag at the gate without having purchased one must have it placed in the hold, for a fee of EUR 60 payable at the gate.
5. If overhead space runs out, cabin bags of Classic, Flex and Business passengers may be placed in the hold at the gate at no charge.

## S04 Checked baggage

1. Checked baggage allowance depends on the fare family (S02). The weight limit per bag is 27 kg (Business: 32 kg).
2. Standard size limit: the sum of length, width and height must not exceed 158 cm.
3. Extra checked bag (up to 27 kg): EUR 35 per bag per flight when purchased online at least 6 hours before departure; EUR 70 per bag per flight when purchased at the airport.
4. Overweight: a bag heavier than its allowance weight, up to 32 kg, costs EUR 50 per bag per flight. Bags heavier than 32 kg are not accepted as checked baggage on any fare family and must be sent as cargo.
5. Oversize: a bag whose dimensions total more than 158 cm and up to 203 cm costs EUR 75 per bag per flight. Bags over 203 cm are not accepted.
6. Overweight and oversize fees are cumulative.
7. Sports equipment: bicycles, golf bags and ski equipment count as one standard checked bag provided they are within the weight limit; the 158 cm size limit does not apply to them. Surfboards and kitesurf boards always cost EUR 80 per flight, regardless of the passenger's allowance.
8. Infants: one checked bag of up to 10 kg plus one foldable stroller, free of charge.
9. Corvina Club Gold members receive one additional checked bag of up to 27 kg on all fare families, including Light.
10. Mobility devices are covered in S11 and do not count towards the allowance.

## S05 Voluntary changes and refunds

1. Light: no changes and no refund. Unused government taxes and airport charges may be refunded on request within 6 months of the scheduled departure date of the unused flight. A processing fee of EUR 15 is deducted; if the refundable taxes are EUR 15 or less, no refund is paid.
2. Classic changes: allowed up to 3 hours before departure, for a fee of EUR 45 per passenger per direction, plus any fare difference.
3. Classic refunds: allowed if requested at least 24 hours before departure, with a fee of EUR 90 per passenger deducted. After that, only taxes are refundable, under the same conditions as Light.
4. Flex: free changes up to 2 hours before departure (fare difference applies). Full refund up to 2 hours before departure. After that, or in case of no-show, the refund is reduced by EUR 50.
5. Business: free changes and a full refund up to the scheduled departure time. In case of no-show, the refund is reduced by EUR 100.
6. On all fare families, if the new fare is lower than the original fare, the difference is not refunded.
7. Refunds are paid to the original form of payment within 14 days. Alternatively, the passenger may choose a travel voucher worth 110% of the refund amount, valid for 12 months from issue and usable on Corvina-operated flights only.

## S06 Delays

1. Care: when the departure delay is expected to reach 2 hours or more (short-haul), 3 hours or more (medium-haul) or 4 hours or more (long-haul), Corvina provides meal vouchers worth EUR 15 per passenger.
2. If the delay requires an overnight stay, Corvina provides hotel accommodation and transport between the airport and the hotel.
3. Compensation is paid for arrival delays caused by circumstances within Corvina's control:
   - Short-haul, arrival delay of 3 hours or more: EUR 200.
   - Medium-haul, arrival delay of 3 hours or more: EUR 350.
   - Long-haul, arrival delay of 3 hours to less than 4 hours: EUR 250.
   - Long-haul, arrival delay of 4 hours or more: EUR 500.
4. Extraordinary circumstances - severe weather, air traffic control restrictions or strikes, security risks, political instability and bird strikes - do not give a right to compensation, but care (items 1 and 2) is still provided. Strikes by Corvina's own staff and technical faults are within Corvina's control.
5. If the departure delay reaches 5 hours or more, the passenger may choose not to travel and receive a full refund of the unused ticket and, where relevant, a return flight to the first point of departure. If the passenger chooses not to travel, compensation under item 3 is not payable.
6. For connecting flights on a single booking, the delay is measured at the final destination.
7. Claims must be submitted through the online form within 12 months of the flight date. Compensation is paid by bank transfer within 30 days or, if the passenger prefers, as a travel voucher worth 130% of the amount, valid for 12 months.

## S07 Cancellations by Corvina

1. When Corvina cancels a flight, the passenger may choose a full refund, paid within 7 days, or free rebooking on the next available Corvina flight. If the next available Corvina flight departs more than 24 hours after the original departure, the passenger may request rebooking on a partner airline.
2. Compensation is paid at the amounts for the distance band: short-haul EUR 200, medium-haul EUR 350, long-haul EUR 500, except in the following cases:
   - The passenger was notified at least 14 days before departure.
   - The passenger was notified 7 to 13 days before departure and the alternative flight departs no more than 2 hours earlier and arrives no more than 4 hours later than the original flight.
   - The passenger was notified less than 7 days before departure and the alternative flight departs no more than 1 hour earlier and arrives no more than 2 hours later than the original flight.
   - The cancellation was caused by extraordinary circumstances (S06).
3. While waiting for an alternative flight, passengers receive care as described in S06.

## S08 Denied boarding and downgrading

1. When a flight is overbooked, Corvina first asks for volunteers to give up their seats in exchange for benefits agreed with each volunteer.
2. Passengers denied boarding against their will receive compensation paid at the airport - short-haul EUR 300, medium-haul EUR 450, long-haul EUR 600 - plus the choice of a refund or rebooking (S07) and care (S06).
3. It is not denied boarding if the passenger reached the gate after it closed (S09), did not present valid travel documents, or was refused for reasons of safety, health or behaviour.
4. Downgrading: a passenger placed in a lower cabin than the one booked receives a refund of 30% (short-haul), 50% (medium-haul) or 75% (long-haul) of the ticket price for that flight, paid within 7 days.

## S09 Check-in and boarding

1. Online check-in opens 36 hours before departure and closes 3 hours before departure.
2. Airport check-in and bag drop close 60 minutes before departure for short- and medium-haul flights, and 75 minutes before departure for long-haul flights.
3. The boarding gate closes 25 minutes before departure for short- and medium-haul flights, and 40 minutes before departure for long-haul flights.
4. A passenger who misses the check-in deadline or the gate closure is a no-show. For Light and Classic fares, a no-show on any flight cancels all remaining flights on the booking. For Flex and Business fares, the remaining flights are kept if the passenger informs Corvina within 24 hours of the missed departure.
5. Priority check-in is available to Business passengers and to Corvina Club Silver and Gold members.

## S10 Travelling with pets

1. In the cabin: cats and dogs only. The combined weight of pet and carrier must not exceed 8 kg, the carrier must not exceed 45 x 30 x 25 cm and must stay under the seat for the whole flight, and the pet must be at least 12 weeks old. A maximum of 3 pets are accepted in the cabin per flight, with one carrier per passenger. Fee: EUR 55 per flight. Pets are not accepted in the cabin on long-haul flights.
2. The pet carrier counts as the passenger's cabin bag. Light passengers, who have no cabin bag allowance, may bring the pet carrier in addition to their personal item. The pet fee applies in all cases.
3. In the hold: cats and dogs up to 45 kg including the crate. Fee: EUR 120 per flight. Pets are not accepted in the hold between 15 June and 15 September (inclusive) on flights to or from Tunis-Carthage (TUN). Brachycephalic (flat-faced) breeds such as pugs, bulldogs (including French bulldogs) and Persian cats are never accepted in the hold.
4. Pets must be booked by phone at least 48 hours before departure.
5. Trained assistance dogs travel free of charge in the cabin on all flights, including long-haul, provided documentation is submitted at least 48 hours before departure. Emotional support animals are not treated as assistance animals; the pet rules above apply to them.

## S11 Special assistance and medical conditions

1. Special assistance (for example wheelchair assistance) is free of charge and should be requested at least 48 hours before departure. Requests made later will be handled where possible but cannot be guaranteed.
2. Up to 2 mobility devices per passenger are carried free of charge in the hold and do not count towards the baggage allowance.
3. Battery-powered wheelchairs must be notified at least 48 hours before departure. Lithium-ion batteries must not exceed 300 Wh.
4. Personal oxygen concentrators are permitted with Corvina's approval. The approval form must be submitted at least 72 hours before departure, and the passenger must carry batteries sufficient for 150% of the scheduled flight duration.
5. Pregnancy:
   - Up to the end of week 28: no restriction.
   - Single pregnancy, from week 29 to the end of week 35: a medical certificate issued no more than 7 days before departure is required. Not accepted from week 36.
   - Multiple pregnancy, from week 29 to the end of week 31: a medical certificate issued no more than 7 days before departure is required. Not accepted from week 32.

## S12 Children and unaccompanied minors

1. An infant travels on an adult's lap and pays 10% of the adult fare plus taxes. Each adult may travel with only one lap infant; a second infant must have their own seat at the child fare.
2. Children (2 to 11) pay 80% of the adult fare on Classic, Flex and Business, and the full adult fare on Light.
3. Unaccompanied minor (UM) service: mandatory for children aged 5 to 11 travelling without a passenger aged 16 or over; optional for children aged 12 to 15. Fee: EUR 70 per direction.
4. Children under 5 cannot travel without a passenger aged 16 or over.
5. On connecting itineraries, the UM service is available only when the connection is at Tunis-Carthage (TUN) with a connection time of at least 2 hours.

## S13 Delayed, lost and damaged baggage

1. A delayed or missing bag must be reported before leaving the baggage reclaim area by filing a Property Irregularity Report (PIR). If that is not possible, it must be reported online within 24 hours of arrival.
2. Interim expenses for essential items while a bag is delayed are reimbursed up to EUR 40 per day for a maximum of 5 days, on presentation of receipts. Interim expenses are not payable when the flight arrived at the passenger's place of residence.
3. Claims for interim expenses must be submitted within 21 days after the bag is delivered.
4. Damage must be reported within 7 days of receiving the bag. Minor damage from normal handling (scratches, scuffs, small dents and small tears) is not covered. Damage to wheels, handles and straps is covered only if the bag becomes unusable.
5. A bag not found within 21 days after the flight's arrival is considered lost, and a lost bag claim can then be made.
6. Corvina's liability is limited to EUR 1,500 per passenger for lost, damaged and delayed baggage combined. A special declaration of higher value made at check-in, for a fee of EUR 25 per bag, raises the limit for that bag to EUR 3,000.
7. Corvina is not liable for fragile items, cash, jewellery, electronics, medicines or important documents packed in checked baggage, unless they were covered by a special declaration.

## S14 Corvina Club loyalty programme

1. Tiers: Blue (entry level), Silver (30,000 points earned in a calendar year) and Gold (70,000 points earned in a calendar year). Silver and Gold status is valid until the end of the following calendar year.
2. Earning: points per EUR of base fare, excluding taxes and charges - Light 4, Classic 8, Flex 10, Business 15. Silver members earn 25% bonus points and Gold members 50% bonus points on flights.
3. Expiry: for Blue members, points expire 24 months after the month in which they were earned. Points do not expire while the member holds Silver or Gold status.
4. Silver benefits: priority check-in, priority baggage handling, 25% bonus points.
5. Gold benefits: priority check-in, priority boarding, priority baggage handling, one additional 27 kg checked bag (S04), lounge access for the member plus one guest on medium- and long-haul departures, free standard seat selection, 50% bonus points.
6. Award tickets are paid in points, with taxes and charges paid in cash. Changing an award ticket costs EUR 25. If an award ticket is cancelled more than 24 hours before departure, the points are redeposited for a fee of EUR 30 and the taxes are refunded. If it is cancelled within 24 hours of departure or not used, the points are forfeited and only the taxes are refunded.

## S15 Seats

1. Standard seat selection costs EUR 8 per flight. It is free for Flex and Business passengers and for Corvina Club Gold members.
2. Extra legroom seats cost EUR 25 (short-haul), EUR 35 (medium-haul) or EUR 50 (long-haul) per flight for all passengers in the economy cabin, including Flex passengers and Gold members.
3. Passengers who do not select a seat are assigned one free of charge at check-in.
4. Exit row seats are available only to passengers aged 16 or over who are able and willing to assist in an evacuation, who are not travelling with an infant or a pet in the cabin, and who have not requested special assistance.
5. If Corvina moves a passenger from a paid seat to a seat of a lower type, the seat fee is refunded. If the passenger is moved to a seat of the same type, no refund is due.
6. Children under 12 on the same booking as an adult are seated next to at least one accompanying adult free of charge, on all fare families including Light.
